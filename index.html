Air-Fried Broccoli and Cauliflower Salad

25 min

Prep 5 min · Soak 5–10 min · Air fry 10–12 min

  1. Boil water in a kettle, then pour it over your broccoli and cauliflower until submerged for 5–10 mins.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 200 degrees.
  3. Drain the florets, then pat them quickly with a kitchen towel to remove excess moisture.
  4. Air fry for 10–12 minutes or until lightly coloured. I like mine a little charred.
  5. Sprinkle a little salt. That's it.
  6. Optional: Add seeds of your choice - I like sesame or sunflower seeds. Enjoy with mustard or just as it is.

Mediterranean Salads

Simple concept: vegetables + healthy fats + vinaigrette dressing

Switch veggies to your liking - cucumber, red/green/yellow peppers, and red or white onion are the base. Add tomatoes, cherry tomatoes, carrots, avocados, radishes, rocket leaves. Whatever you want.

Lentil Salad

15 min

Prep 15 min · No cooking

  1. Drain and rinse the cooked canned lentils, pat them dry with a paper towel (better but optional), and then transfer them to a large mixing bowl.
  2. Finely dice the cucumber, red bell pepper, and red onion.
  3. Chop the fresh coriander leaves and mint.
  4. Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, honey, minced garlic, salt, and pepper in a small bowl or jar until smooth.
  5. Add the diced vegetables, chopped herbs, and feta cheese (it's naturally salty so adjust salt accordingly) or cottage cheese (optional) to the lentils, drizzle with the dressing, and toss everything together until well combined.

Chickpea Salad

15 min

Prep 15 min · No cooking

  1. Drain and rinse the cooked canned chickpeas, pat them dry with a paper towel (better but optional), and then transfer them to a large mixing bowl.
  2. Finely dice the cucumber, red bell pepper, red onion, and halved Kalamata olives (optional).
  3. Chop the fresh coriander (or parsley) leaves and mint (optional).
  4. Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, white wine vinegar (optional), crushed red pepper flakes (optional), minced garlic (optional), salt, and pepper in a small bowl or jar until smooth.
  5. Add the diced vegetables, chopped herbs, and feta cheese (it's naturally salty so adjust salt accordingly) (optional) to the chickpeas, drizzle with the dressing, and toss everything together until well combined.

Zucchini Salad

25 min

Prep 10 min · Air fry 10–12 min

  1. Slice the zucchini and toss in a bowl with 1 to 2 tablespoons of olive oil, salt, pepper, and dried oregano or cumin.
  2. Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C), add the zucchini in a single layer, and air fry for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking halfway through until tender.
  3. Toss cherry tomatoes into the air fryer basket for the last 3 to 4 minutes of cooking (optional).
  4. Transfer the warm air-fried zucchini and tomatoes to a bowl, then add the sliced red onion and Kalamata olives (optional).
  5. Add the fresh parsley (or coriander) and mint (optional), drizzle with the olive oil and lemon juice, add the feta cheese (it's naturally salty so adjust salt accordingly) (optional), then toss everything together until well combined.

Sandwich

20 min

Prep 10 min · Cook 10 min

  1. Lightly toast two slices of your favourite bread. (This bread seems nice: https://www.walmart.ca/en/ip/Country-Harvest-14-Grains-Protein-Sliced-Bread-12g-Protein-Per-Serving-No-Artificial-Preservatives-Flavours-or-Colours-600g/5LYQF6C8BTWJ?from=/search)
  2. Spread a layer of pesto sauce evenly over one side of one bread slice.
  3. Spread or layer sliced avocado (or mashed avocado) over the other slice of bread.
  4. Layer your choice of flavoured (change the flavour, and it's literally a brand-new sandwich) canned tuna evenly over the avocado base. (https://www.walmart.ca/en/c/kp/canned-tuna)
  5. Top with cucumber slices, tomato slices, and red onion rings.
  6. Add a single fried egg, press the two bread slices together, and serve.

Variations

Swap the tuna for air-fried paneer or tofu.
